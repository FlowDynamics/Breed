In deep orbit above hostile worlds, silence shatters aboard drifting starships. In BREED, you command a biomechanical alien brood against heavily armed Space Marines, Pyro Troopers, Auto-Turrets, and Siege Tanks!

TWO PHASES – 100% DETERMINISTIC TACTICS
• Phase 1 (Planning): Pick your alien species along the bottom dock, deploy them onto pulsing Creep tiles, and pre-program every step—either by tapping highlighted tiles directly on the room grid or via the command bar.
• Phase 2 (Auto-Combat): Launch the assault and watch smooth, deterministic chess-like rounds unfold!

SWITCH PUZZLES, COUNTERS & OUTNUMBERING
• Use air Vents to flank enemies, trigger Consoles to lock armored bulkhead Doors, cut Deck Lights, or power down Auto-Turrets, and detonate explosive Acid Barrels.
• Victory requires both swarm outnumbering (2v1 / 3v1 bonuses) and smart unit counters (Acid Spitters melt Siege Tank armor; Stalkers strike hard from shadows).
• Dynamic Day & Night cycles alter sensor ranges, shadow ambush damage, and Egg hatching speed.

SPACESHIP CAMPAIGN & ROOM LOSS
• Conquer 8 handcrafted rooms across Freighter Nostromo-IX (Acheron IV) and Cruiser Kryon-Bastion (Kryon VII).
• Upgrade conquered rooms and unlock dedicated per-species mutation trees & Alpha evolutions using Mutagens.
• Real stakes: if hostiles survive or escape through EXIT, you lose a previously conquered room!

BUILT-IN LEVEL EDITOR
• Design your own portrait tactical rooms with doors, vents, tanks, patrol routes, and Day/Night cycles, playtest live, and share via JSON.

FREE TO PLAY (ENGLISH & GERMAN)
• The full base campaign (8 rooms), 5 core alien species, and Level Editor are completely free and playable offline.
• Optional one-time in-app purchases in the Shop unlock Elite Aliens (Blade Reaper, Spore Bomber) and permanent Bio-Weapons.
